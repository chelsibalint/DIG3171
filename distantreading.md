# Distant Reading Assignment 

I used Voyant to search the following website. [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/345/pg345.txt)

I've never used Voyant before, so I wasn't too certain what I was getting into. It allows the reader to quickly analyze a large amount of texts without reading the entire document. It organizes the text into visual patterns and statistics. You can see the most used terms and where certain words appear most frequently. Voyant doesn't identify the intention or tone behind the word choices however.
